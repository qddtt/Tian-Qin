These projects connect my field engineering experience with graduate research in control and autonomy, from mine-site deployment and real-vehicle calibration to model-based and learning-oriented control.

## Autonomous Transportation and Loading/Unloading System for Large Open-Pit Mine Robots

- **Program:** National Key Research and Development Program of China, 2022-2025.
- **Goal:** Develop key technologies for autonomous transportation and loading/unloading in large open-pit mine robot systems.
- **Methods:** Adaptive/fuzzy PID longitudinal control for low-speed autonomous mining trucks, load-adaptive MPC for lateral trajectory tracking, vehicle dynamics modeling, and robustness evaluation under variable-payload operating conditions.
- **Research value:** This work frames my next research step around model calibration, parameter identification, and learning/data-driven control for safety-critical autonomous systems.

## rbMPC-truck

[GitHub repository](https://github.com/qddtt/rbMPC-truck)

- **Goal:** Reproduce and migrate Model Predictive Control algorithms for autonomous mining truck trajectory tracking.
- **Context:** Heavy-duty vehicles operate with large inertia, changing payloads, and terrain constraints.
- **Methods:** MPC formulation, trajectory tracking evaluation, controller comparison, and code migration.
- **Research value:** This project supports my transition from classical model-based control toward learning-enhanced control for autonomous systems.

## Unmanned Mining Truck Control System

- **Role:** Hardware Engineer, CASIC, 2019-2023.
- **Goal:** Support stable operation and retrofit adaptation for industrial vehicle-control systems in field environments.
- **Methods:** Engine-throttle calibration, joint debugging among vehicle controllers, sensors, and actuators, control-parameter/interface-configuration adjustment, on-site testing, data logging, and issue reproduction.
- **Context:** Calibration work under response lag, communication anomalies, and on-site adaptation constraints.
- **Research value:** This experience motivates my interest in autonomy methods that respect real deployment constraints rather than only simulation performance.

## Field Calibration and Safety Control for Autonomous Mining Trucks

- **Role:** Control Algorithm Engineer, Mindway Science and Technology Co., Ltd., 2025-present.
- **Goal:** Develop and validate control and safety logic for autonomous mining trucks on real-vehicle platforms.
- **Methods:** LQR lateral control, dual-loop PID longitudinal speed/braking control, path-tracking and braking validation, AEB anti-collision logic based on time-domain trajectory prediction and OBB detection, and two-degree-of-freedom vehicle model identification from real-vehicle data.
- **Research value:** Strengthened my interest in combining model-based reliability with adaptive, data-driven methods that remain robust under field mismatch.

## Quantitative Stock Prediction and Trading Strategy

- **Type:** Independent machine-learning project.
- **Goal:** Build a small prediction-to-decision pipeline from historical market data to trading signals.
- **Methods:** Feature engineering, MLP and random-forest prediction models, weighted ensemble outputs, and rule-based buy/sell strategy conversion.
- **Result:** Backtested return of 13% and directional prediction accuracy of 55% over the selected test period.
- **Research value:** Provided hands-on practice with data-driven modeling, ensemble prediction, and decision rules outside the vehicle-control domain.

## Autopilot Challenge of Vehicle-Road-Cloud Integration 2024

- **Goal:** Deploy controller logic on a real autonomous vehicle platform for parking, obstacle avoidance, lane-changing, and U-turn tasks.
- **Methods:** LQR controller integration, system testing, and scenario-level debugging.
- **Result:** Innovation Award (Top 5%, ranked 7th among 118 teams).
- **Research value:** Strengthened my understanding of the gap between controller design and real vehicle execution.

## CICV 2023 Adaptive Cruise Controller

- **Goal:** Build an adaptive cruise control strategy in the Virtual Test Drive simulation platform.
- **Methods:** Dynamic Window Approach and fuzzy PID hybrid control.
- **Result:** Contribution Award (Top 5%); lateral acceleration RMS below 0.1 m/s<sup>2</sup> and longitudinal acceleration RMS below 0.06 m/s<sup>2</sup>.
- **Research value:** Provided an early bridge between motion planning and adaptive control.
