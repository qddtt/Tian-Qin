These notes make my current research questions explicit. I use them to separate established control knowledge from working assumptions, connect methods to deployment constraints, and define experiments that could turn an idea into evidence.

My starting point is model-based control, vehicle dynamics, system identification, and field autonomy. The direction I am exploring is how reinforcement learning and data-driven models can extend that foundation without discarding constraints, interpretability, or reliable fallback behavior.

Each article is labeled according to its maturity. A research concept describes a proposed architecture and validation path; it is not presented as a completed method or experimental result.
