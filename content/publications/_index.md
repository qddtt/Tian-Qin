My publications focus on control algorithms for autonomous vehicles and mining trucks, especially under tracking, payload, and robustness constraints.

## Journal

**Qin T**, Zhu D, Wang C, et al. *Dual-Loop Fuzzy-PID Acceleration Tracking Controller for Autonomous Mining Trucks under Variable Payload Conditions*. **Coal Engineering**, 2026 (in press).

- **Problem:** Longitudinal acceleration tracking becomes unstable when autonomous mining trucks operate under variable payload conditions.
- **Method:** Dual-loop fuzzy-PID controller with self-adaptive compensation.
- **Result:** Maximum speed error of 0.49 km/h, 8.77% improvement in peak-to-peak speed error, and 13.30% improvement in acceleration error compared with pure MPC.
- **My contribution:** Controller design, experiment analysis, and paper preparation.

## Conference

**Qin T**, Qiu L, Chen J, et al. *Double-Layer Following Controller for Autonomous Vehicles*. **CCDC 2024**, IEEE, pp. 908-913.

- **Problem:** Following control needs to balance tracking accuracy and comfort in autonomous driving scenarios.
- **Method:** Dynamic Window Approach combined with a fuzzy-adaptive PID controller.
- **Result:** 19.5% reduction in lateral acceleration RMS compared with PID-Stanley.
- **My contribution:** Controller development, simulation evaluation, and manuscript writing.

Wang C, Zhu D, **Qin T**, et al. *Lateral Trajectory Tracking of Autonomous Mining Trucks Using MPC with Adaptive Load Compensation*. **CCC 2025**, IEEE.

- **Problem:** Lateral tracking performance degrades under large payload variation in mining truck operation.
- **Method:** MPC with adaptive load compensation for trajectory tracking.
- **Result:** 33.33% reduction in mean lateral error compared with LQR.
- **My contribution:** Research discussion, experiment support, and mining truck control context.

Yang P, Zhu D, **Qin T**, et al. *Adaptive Heading Tracking Algorithm Based on Vehicle Dynamics Model*. **CCC 2025**, IEEE.

- **Problem:** Pure pursuit tracking can lose precision when vehicle dynamics and heading response are not adequately modeled.
- **Method:** Adaptive heading tracking algorithm based on a vehicle dynamics model.
- **Result:** 47% reduction in lateral error compared with pure pursuit.
- **My contribution:** Research discussion, vehicle dynamics context, and paper support.
